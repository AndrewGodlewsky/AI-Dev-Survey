Glossary — the terms on this page(click to open)

The scores (questions 1, 2 and 4)

Dimension
One independently scored 1–5 scale. There are seven personal ones, and deliberately no single master "AI level" — each stands alone. A high score is a position, not a grade: Guardrails high ≠ responsible, Concern high ≠ negative.
The seven Dimensions
Comfort — willingness to use AI in one's own development work. Trust — confidence that AI output is correct and safe without line-by-line scrutiny. Fluency — practical skill with AI tools: prompting, agent workflows, setup, failure modes. Guardrails — how much review, disclosure, testing and policy someone wants around AI-assisted work. Concern — perceived risk: security, IP/licensing, skill atrophy, job impact, maintainability (5 = most concerned). Autonomy — how unsupervised AI may act on someone's behalf: autocomplete → chat → supervised agent → autonomous agent. Craft values — weight placed on general development principles (understanding every line, tests, review, ownership); a lens for reading the others, not an adoption metric.
Current score · Direction score
Where someone is today on a Dimension, and how much they would want to work a given way — appetite, never a forecast. Both are decimals; the nearest whole number is a landmark pointing at the rubric's prose, never the score itself.
Gap
Direction minus Current, per Dimension. Positive = would want more of it than practised today; negative = would pull back. Appetite minus practice — a Gap of 0.3 is real, and its direction matters more than its size.
Team mean · range · n
A team number is the mean plus the range (highest minus lowest) plus every named value — no standard deviation at this team size. n is how many people that number is built from.

The work grid (question 3)

Coverage
Not a score but a profile: an Involvement level per area of work, asked twice — today (how you do it now) and direction (where you want AI). The chart shows the team median per pass; the number at the right is the median within-person move.
Involvement level
1 None (no AI) · 2 Advisor (AI explains, I do the work) · 3 Drafter (AI drafts, I rework every line) · 4 Doer, reviewed (AI does it, I review the result) · 5 Doer, spot-checked · 6 System-verified (AI does it end-to-end; automated checks catch errors). N/A = I don't do this work — excluded, never averaged in.

The norms (question 5)

Team Dimension · Norm score
A Dimension about how the team should work, answered once in team voice. Each person's Norm score is the norm they would set; the spread across people is the finding, not the mean. The direction is a direction, not the good direction.
The five Team Dimensions
Transparency — how much the team requires AI's role in work to be visible and personally owned. Verification — how much human review the team requires of AI output. Agent latitude — how freely agents may act in the shared codebase (its two facets, Parallel development and Limits, are the primary read — a middling 3 can hide opposite positions). Pace — how fast the team adopts, and who sets that pace. Shared practice — how much the team invests in shared AI setup and in keeping everyone able to work without AI.
Reverse-worded (↺)
A statement worded so that agreeing means less of the Dimension; its answer is flipped before scoring. The bars here show the raw answer as given.

Beside the numbers (in the terminal and the CSV download, not on this page)

Safeguards · Lever · Hold · Dropped
Three identical tick-lists ask what counts as enough for AI-written output — today, would-be-willing, and as a team norm. A Lever is a check ticked in willing but not today; a Hold is a reading position ticked in both (the line does not move); a Dropped is ticked today but not in willing. Positions, never scores.
Edge marker
Raised when someone answers a deliberately extreme probe statement at 4–5, or reports Involvement level 6 on a high-stakes area. It locates a position; it does not report a fault.
Pattern flag
Raised when a block of answers is flat in a way its reverse-worded items don't support. It locates a conversation to have — never a fault, and it changes no numbers.
#N/A
A blank scored answer is a malformed export, not data: the affected score reads #N/A visibly and nothing is averaged around it. Recovery is a conversation, not a formula.
Initials
A display choice, not anonymity — hover shows the full name, and results are shared openly within the team. Nothing leaves the team as a file; anything shared onward is a screenshot chosen together.
